## Supported interpretation

- The native continuation decrement is-1.200pp in this smaller conditional subset, but its interval crosses zero. This is not a fresh replication of the original32-parent primary, nor evidence that continuation is harmless.
- Recipient-state contrasts on identical donor data and the recipient-by-donor interaction remain inconclusive. Do not claim that the controls isolate parameter fragility, endogenous data shift, or a causal mediation mechanism. D0 was harvested on DGX and D1 is archived, so their source contrast also includes generation/runtime differences.
- Ten/25-step and pooled corrections show no statistically resolved improvement or deterioration relative to their paired native baseline. Wide intervals do not establish equivalence or rule out useful recipe effects.
- The gold-reference arm is11.556pp worse than the matched verified-self-generated arm under this exact recipe; its simultaneous interval excludes zero. This does not show reference solutions are inherently worse. Equal examples/optimizer steps do not match label-token exposure, text style or training losses. Audit saved batches, output format and retokenized lengths before building a mechanism claim around this unexpected effect.
