## Fixed questions and claim hierarchy

The original H1 learned admission rule remains the provisional method primary.
Its development mean must reach the 0.5pp practical target in both whole-parent
and held-seed views before a powered method-efficacy confirmation is eligible.
The positive exploratory backbone-H2 baseline cannot silently replace it as a
novel learned controller.

If that method criterion is unmet, evaluate an explicitly narrower **measurement
confirmation** before spending on new data. Its two directional replication
endpoints are (1) pooled forced candidate-plus-continuation utility below hold,
and (2) Qwen-minus-Llama forced-update utility, averaging math/code equally.
Report both model means and all four streams; pooled harm is not universal harm.
This is a fresh empirical test of the observed interaction, not method efficacy.

Any ensuing measurement confirmation must freeze H1/H2 ridge, H1/H2 backbone
calibration, protected replay, forced update and hold before outcomes. Future
supervision benefit and admission effectiveness remain separate secondary
comparisons, with an explicit multiplicity family. A favorable simple baseline
is not automatically a novel algorithm. No extra architecture or scale search.

## Design calculation

Bind the 16-parent B plan/report, decision audit report/predictions, all four
timing reports, and checked fixture classification/completion to hashes. Recheck
item/checkpoint provenance on allocated CPUs. Use orientation zero consistently:
the fixed first hash half supplies selection, and its disjoint second half
supplies pilot scoring. Never choose a favorable orientation. Preserve historical
two-orientation summaries; this is a forward design sensitivity.

Project balanced N=48,64,32 in that preference order. For each, price target/OOD
counts 128,256,384, limited by independently available role counts, with retention
256. Shared scoring identities resample together across parents; resample whole
parents within the four fixed streams. Use 2,000 draws, fixed candidate actions
and three native continuation draws. Do not refit or treat draws/candidates as N.

For measurement, examine pilot-effect shrinkage 0.5/0.75/1 and SD inflation
1/1.25/1.5. Eligibility requires at least 80% conditional planned joint power at
0.75 shrinkage and 1.25 SD inflation for both endpoints, using the conservative
normal critical value at 1−0.05/4. For method planning, use a 0.5pp effect above
the better constant, preserving the resulting asymmetric margins against forced
and hold; report retention precision separately. These are empirical planning
assumptions, not guaranteed real-data power. Code development OOD differs from
final OOD, and model/parent/item-distribution shifts are unresolved.

Choose the least costly eligible item configuration at the first eligible N.
Require complete conservative reservations plus CPU overhead to fit 810 SUs and
leave at least 100 SUs of total project headroom. The possible 810-SU C ceiling
comes from dropping optional D and transferring its 250-SU ceiling, before any
C outcomes; this transfer must be recorded before C submission. It does not
raise the 1,200-SU project cap. No qualifying design means stop GPU expansion.
