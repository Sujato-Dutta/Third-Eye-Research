# Paper evidence plan through October 11

The strongest feasible contribution is an empirical study of the reliability
and selection cost of persistent parameter self-updates. Architectural superiority
is not established by the current results. The headline must follow replicated
effects rather than the original forecaster proposal or raw reversal counts.

The deadline package is fixed: complete the three original checkpoint recoveries;
collect eight balanced five-generation trajectories; run matched optimizer and
continuation controls on eight independent saved parents; and evaluate previously
frozen methods without fitting or selecting using the new cohort. A separately
declared four-state SmolLM3 sanity check was subsequently authorized under S1;
it is excluded from E2's primary reliability and forecasting population. No
architecture search, full third-backbone recursive campaign or learned online
rollout is scheduled.

## Evidence needed for claims

| Question | Evidence | Claim boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Do immediate and future selections differ meaningfully? | Native labels, aligned checkpoint reevaluations, paired item uncertainty, matched optimizer repeats and fresh continuation seeds | Raw disagreement alone does not show a horizon effect beyond noise |
| What does immediate selection cost? | Greedy versus random, observed future oracle and holding parameters fixed; selection regret with trajectory intervals | Observed oracle is privileged and finite-evaluation optimistic; forced update differs from abstention |
| Does forecasting generalize? | Fixed Direct-H2 versus matched-H1 primary comparison, all frozen neural/ridge/tree controls, balanced fresh cohort | Eight trajectories limit power; unsuccessful tested methods do not imply intrinsic unpredictability |
| Can the test detect a signal? | Existing known-signal ridge and independent-GRU controls, fixed learning curves | Constructed-signal recovery does not establish power for every real effect |
| Why do rankings change? | Frozen update-norm, retention-gradient, diversity and NLL associations, replicated with uncertainty | Associations are descriptive; current intervals do not establish a mechanism |
| Does the conclusion cover recursive improvement generally? | Two backbones, two task families, persistent LoRA updates, H=2 labels along T=5 random trajectories | No universal claim, full-parameter claim, long-horizon forecast claim or learned-policy online gain |

Equal-weight four-stream confirmation and independent-trajectory intervals are
essential. Forty states and120 candidate labels are not120 independent experimental
units. The reliability supplement has eight independent parents, two per stream;
the shared evaluation sets introduce an additional dependence limitation.

## Direction after the fixed review

If balanced replication and matched controls support a meaningful future-selection
cost beyond the measured sensitivity, lead with that empirical reliability result
and place forecasting as a secondary, carefully bounded experiment. A possible
title, contingent on that evidence, is **“Beyond Immediate Gains: Measuring the
Reliability of Recursive Parameter Self-Updates.”**

An architectural headline needs a clear fresh-cohort advantage for the frozen
method against matched H1 and simple controls; no new architecture can be selected
using this cohort. If the reliability effect is small, unresolved or mostly within
measured variation, narrow the claim to the tested setting and its reproducibility
limits. Do not force a positive headline to meet a venue target. Main versus a
more limited empirical submission must follow the final evidence and its strength;
neither Main nor Oral acceptance can be guaranteed.
