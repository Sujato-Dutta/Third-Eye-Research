# Final empirical confirmation and evidence freeze

Declared October 22 before this cohort's fresh outcomes. This is the final
collection for the present empirical paper, followed by an automatic results
package. No forecaster is fitted, compared or restored as the study's objective.
Historical forecasting failures remain negative background evidence.

## Why this primary question

The completed design review found no affordable, adequately powered design for
the proposed learned-method claim or its joint pooled-harm/interaction endpoint.
Preserve that report and its stop decision. A pooled negative mean is unnecessary
for establishing heterogeneous update effects: useful updates in one backbone
can offset harmful updates in another. Before fresh data, narrow the primary to
**the difference in future forced-update utility between the two fixed models**.
This is an empirical replication, not successful forecasting or a new controller.

The single primary is Qwen-minus-Llama candidate-plus-standardized-continuation
utility, averaging math/code equally, under the fixed correction protocol.
Use a two-sided joint parent/shared-item bootstrap 95% interval. Inference is
over update seeds and items for these two fixed backbones, not a population of
model families or a randomized causal effect of model identity. A positive gap
does not by itself establish a benefit/harm crossover; that stronger statement
requires simultaneous Qwen-positive and Llama-negative model intervals.

Compute conditional planned power for this exact single endpoint before data:
32 independent parents, effect 75% of the orientation-zero pilot estimate,
SD inflated 1.25, normal critical 1.96, 2,000 parent/shared-item resamples.
Require at least 80% under those assumptions. Preserve the prior unsuccessful
two-endpoint calculation; do not call it passed. Planning is conditional on a
small development cohort and code-OOD proxy, not guaranteed real-world power.

## Fixed cohort and outcomes

Collect **32 fresh initial parents**, eight per model/task stream. Disjoint seed
ranges are 2026102200–2026102207 (Llama code), 2026102300–2026102307 (Llama math),
2026102400–2026102407 (Qwen code), and 2026102500–2026102507 (Qwen math). Audit
collisions against surviving run metadata before collection. No favorable seed
replacement, resizing or adaptive stopping from outcomes.

Keep K=3, size-two unique verified corrections per candidate, eight harvest
attempts, stride 16, 50 optimizer steps, rank16/LR1e-4 and frozen date/rendering.
Each candidate has three native continuation draws, original plus offsets
50,000,000 and100,000,000. Scarcity remains terminal M2=M1 with zero continuation
steps; initial candidate infeasibility remains a recorded failure, not a cloned
correction or replaced parent. Maximum training is 384 updates before terminal
skips; 416 checkpoint evaluations are planned.

Freeze **128 target,128 OOD,256 retention** independent scoring examples per
task by an outcome-blind identity hash. Preserve original selection suites;
orientation-zero development H1 items alone select candidates and replay actions.
Code scoring OOD is explicitly HumanEval; development OOD is MBPP validation.
The previous eight hash-reserved HumanEval items are cost calibration only and
excluded from scoring. Freeze all manifest hashes/identities before collection.

Evaluate four rules, all selecting the same immediate-gain candidate with uniform
ties: hold; forced commitment; target-positive/protected-OOD-and-retention replay;
and a fixed development-derived family baseline that commits Qwen and holds
Llama. The last is a transparent baseline restricted to these models, not a
trained predictor, transferable method or evidence of novel algorithm design.
Write each parent's actions durably before accessing its final model outcomes.

Score the parent, all three M1 checkpoints and all nine M2 draws: **13 per
parent**. H2 is an observed future outcome, not a forecasting model. Average draws
within parent/candidate; candidates and draws never increase independent N.

## Inference and reporting

Report primary uncertainty, both model means, all streams, all three accuracy
heads, immediate/future utility, retention, coverage, scarcity and costs. Joint
bootstrap resamples whole parents within fixed streams and shares exact item
resamples across models/parents using identical pools. Also report separate
parent/item conditional intervals; do not add them as variance components.
Check shared pool structure before collection and terminal equality again on
scoring outcomes. Require all 32 complete parents for confirmatory analysis.

Freeze one secondary family: family-rule minus hold/forced/replay; replay minus
hold/forced; forced future minus forced immediate. Apply Holm-adjusted centered
crossed-bootstrap p values and simultaneous six-comparison intervals. A practical
family-rule advantage requires positive simultaneous intervals versus both
constants and replay, at least0.5pp above the better constant, and the retention
one-sided95% lower bound above−0.5pp. Otherwise report its limited/absent value.
Do not promote a secondary result as the predeclared primary.

No architecture result or universal harm claim follows from this experiment.
Whatever the outcomes, freeze this cohort and write the supported empirical
paper. Do not automatically add another method, model, intervention or rollout.
